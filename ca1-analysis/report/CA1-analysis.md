# CA1: Requirements and Domain Analysis
## Riverside Community Partnership Equipment Lending Service

## 1. Scenario Interpretation

### 1.1 Organisation

Riverside Community Partnership is a non-profit organisation that lends
equipment for community activities. Its collection includes items such as
tables, chairs, projectors, public-address equipment, outdoor shelters,
sports equipment, first-aid kits and lighting.

### 1.2 Present Process

The current equipment-loan process is mainly managed through telephone calls,
emails, a shared spreadsheet and sometimes handwritten notes.

1. Borrowers submit equipment requests by telephone or email. Telephone
   requests may first be written in a notebook and transferred to the shared
   spreadsheet later.

2. Staff identify whether the requester is an existing group or a new group.
   A new group may need to explain its purpose and planned activity.

3. Staff check equipment availability in the shared spreadsheet and may also
   check the storage room.

4. If the full requested quantity is unavailable, staff may offer a lower
   quantity. The borrower may accept the lower quantity or withdraw the request.

5. A request may be approved, rejected, withdrawn or left pending further
   information.

6. Approved equipment is collected from the partnership office.

7. Before collection, staff locate the request, confirm approved equipment,
   identify the person collecting it, check the equipment, provide any required
   instructions and record the collection.

8. Borrowers return equipment by the agreed return date.

9. Staff inspect returned equipment for quantity, missing components, visible
   damage, cleanliness, safety and maintenance needs.

10. Damage may be recorded in the spreadsheet or reported by email. Repairs may
    be completed by staff or an external repair service.

11. When a borrower requests an extension, staff check whether it would conflict
    with later approved requests.

12. When equipment is overdue and has not been returned by the agreed return date,
staff contact the borrower by telephone or email.


### 1.3 Problems

- P-01: Manual transfer from notebook to spreadsheet may delay or make records incomplete.
- P-02: Equipment names, quantities, dates and statuses are inconsistent.
- P-03: Spreadsheet availability may not match the physical storage room.
- P-04: Request information may be missing because there is no standard request form.
- P-05: Important checking and approval practices are known by experienced staff but are not consistently documented.
- P-06: Eligibility decisions for different borrower types are inconsistent.
- P-07: Deposit reasons and amounts are not standardised.
- P-08: Staff disagree on when equipment becomes reserved.
- P-09: Priority between conflicting requests is not formally documented.
- P-10: Collection exceptions and collection-recording steps are handled inconsistently.
- P-11: Missing components may not be detected when equipment systems are recorded as one item.
- P-12: Uninspected returns create uncertainty about equipment availability.
- P-13: Damage handling and the decision to return equipment to use are not consistently recorded.
- P-14: Repeated or serious late returns have no formally defined consequences.

### 1.4 Problem Consequences

| Problem | Consequence |
|---|---|
| P-01 | C-01: Request records may be delayed or incomplete, which may cause staff to work with missing information. |
| P-02 | C-02: Staff may use inconsistent information when identifying equipment, quantities, dates or statuses. |
| P-03 | C-03: Staff may approve or promise equipment that is not physically available. |
| P-04 | C-04: Staff may need to contact borrowers again, delaying the request decision. |
| P-05 | C-05: Less experienced staff may omit required checks, instructions or coordinator approval. |
| P-06 | C-06: Similar borrower requests may receive inconsistent eligibility decisions. |
| P-07 | C-07: Comparable requests may receive inconsistent deposit decisions. |
| P-08 | C-08: Two borrowers may believe that the same equipment is reserved for them. |
| P-09 | C-09: Staff may be unable to apply or explain priority decisions consistently. |
| P-10 | C-10: Equipment may be released without a consistent record of the collector and collection checks. |
| P-11 | C-11: A complete equipment system may be unusable because a required component is missing. |
| P-12 | C-12: Staff may not know whether returned equipment is available, damaged or missing components. |
| P-13 | C-13: Damaged or unsafe equipment may be returned to use without a clear recorded decision. |
| P-14 | C-14: Staff have no formally defined basis for considering repeated late returns in future decisions. |
