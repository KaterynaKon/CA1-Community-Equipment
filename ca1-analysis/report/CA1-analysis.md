# CA1: Requirements and Domain Analysis
## Riverside Community Partnership Equipment Lending Service

## 1. Scenario Interpretation

### 1.1 Organisation

Riverside Community Partnership is a non-profit organisation that lends
equipment for community activities. Its collection includes items such as
tables, chairs, projectors, public-address equipment, outdoor shelters,
sports equipment, first-aid kits and lighting.

### 1.2 Present Process

The current equipment-loan process is mainly managed through telephone calls,
emails, a shared spreadsheet and sometimes handwritten notes.

1. Borrowers submit equipment requests by telephone or email. Telephone
   requests may first be written in a notebook and transferred to the shared
   spreadsheet later.

2. Staff identify whether the requester is an existing group or a new group.
   A new group may need to explain its purpose and planned activity.

3. Staff check equipment availability in the shared spreadsheet and may also
   check the storage room.

4. If the full requested quantity is unavailable, staff may offer a lower
   quantity. The borrower may accept the lower quantity or withdraw the request.

5. A request may be approved, rejected, withdrawn or left pending further
   information.

6. Approved equipment is collected from the partnership office.

7. Before collection, staff locate the request, confirm approved equipment,
   identify the person collecting it, check the equipment, provide any required
   instructions and record the collection.

8. Borrowers return equipment by the agreed return date.

9. Staff inspect returned equipment for quantity, missing components, visible
   damage, cleanliness, safety and maintenance needs.

10. Damage may be recorded in the spreadsheet or reported by email. Repairs may
    be completed by staff or an external repair service.

11. When a borrower requests an extension, staff check whether it would conflict
    with later approved requests.

12. When equipment is overdue and has not been returned by the agreed return date,
staff contact the borrower by telephone or email.


### 1.3 Problems

- P-01: Manual transfer from notebook to spreadsheet may delay or make records incomplete.
- P-02: Equipment names, quantities, dates and statuses are inconsistent.
- P-03: Spreadsheet availability may not match the physical storage room.
- P-04: Request information may be missing because there is no standard request form.
- P-05: Important checking and approval practices are known by experienced staff but are not consistently documented.
- P-06: Eligibility decisions for different borrower types are inconsistent.
- P-07: Deposit reasons and amounts are not standardised.
- P-08: Staff disagree on when equipment becomes reserved.
- P-09: Priority between conflicting requests is not formally documented.
- P-10: Collection exceptions and collection-recording steps are handled inconsistently.
- P-11: Missing components may not be detected when equipment systems are recorded as one item.
- P-12: Uninspected returns create uncertainty about equipment availability.
- P-13: Damage handling and the decision to return equipment to use are not consistently recorded.
- P-14: Repeated or serious late returns have no formally defined consequences.

### 1.7 Uncertainty

- U-01: At what point, and under which conditions, should equipment become reserved?

- U-02: Which residents, private events and businesses are eligible to borrow equipment?

- U-03: When should a deposit be required, how should its amount be decided,
  and who may approve exceptions?

- U-04: When should returned equipment become available for another borrower?

- U-05: How should repeated late returns or late cancellations affect future
  lending decisions?

- U-06: Which messages, if any, should be sent automatically rather than
  reviewed by staff?

- U-07: How much historical information should be retained or transferred
  into the new service?

## 2. Purpose, Scope and Boundary

### 2.1 Purpose
The purpose of the Riverside Equipment Lending Service is to make the current
equipment-lending process more reliable, consistent, transparent and traceable,
so that staff can manage requests, availability, collection, returns and
equipment condition using clear and current information.

### 2.2 In-Scope Functions

- Recording equipment loan requests.

- Supporting staff to check request information, relevant conditions and
  equipment availability before a person makes a request decision.

- Recording request decisions, including approved, rejected, withdrawn or
  pending further information.

- Recording changes to a request, including changes to requested quantities,
  collection dates, return dates or the named collector.

- Supporting extension requests by checking whether an extension would conflict
  with later approved requests.

- Recording equipment collection for an approved request.

- Recording equipment returns.

- Recording equipment condition, missing components, damage and maintenance needs.

- Showing the current equipment status and availability to authorised staff.


### 2.3 Out-of-Scope Functions

- A shared equipment-lending service operated by multiple organisations.
  The initial scope covers only equipment managed by Riverside Community Partnership.

### 2.4 Dependencies

- D-01: The service depends on staff or authorised volunteers recording
  equipment collection, return and inspection results.

- D-02: The service depends on staff obtaining missing request information
  from borrowers and updating the request record.

- D-03: The service depends on staff physically checking equipment condition,
  quantity and components before recording the inspection outcome.

- D-04: The service depends on authorised staff or coordinators making
  request decisions, including decisions about unusual cases.

- D-05: The service depends on staff contacting borrowers about overdue
  equipment and recording any relevant outcome.


### 2.5 Assumptions

- A-01: It is assumed that Riverside will define authorised user roles before
  implementation. The scenario confirms that volunteers need limited access,
  but the complete role and permission model requires stakeholder confirmation.

- A-02: It is assumed that existing spreadsheet records will be reviewed for
  incomplete entries, inconsistent equipment names and possible duplicates
  before they are relied on by the new service. The amount of historical data
  to retain or migrate remains an open question (U-07).


### 2.6 Constraints

- C-01: Internet access is occasionally unreliable.

- C-02: Riverside has few staff, relies on volunteers, and has limited funding
  and specialist technical support.

- C-03: Volunteers may need to record collections and returns without seeing
  deposit information or unnecessary personal data.

- C-04: Existing records contain incomplete entries, inconsistent equipment
  names and possible duplicates.

- C-05: The initial scope is limited to equipment managed by Riverside
  Community Partnership.
